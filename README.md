# Hireriv – Staffing & Recruitment Website

A complete website for a tech-driven staffing and recruitment company, built with Node.js, Express and EJS.

## Features
- **Design**: blue and white (#123B70 primary blue, #2878D4 action blue, #F3F6FA light background) with the Inter font. Text and buttons use #1F6BC2, a slightly darker action blue, so they pass WCAG AA contrast. Every link, button and field shows a clear focus ring for keyboard users
- **Header**: Hireriv by Raigozen logo, Find Jobs, Job Categories, Staffing Solutions, Employers, Career Resources and Saved, a **Login** menu (job seekers or employers) and a **Post a Job** button (`/post-a-job`: signed-in clients go straight to their new-requirement form, everyone else to the hiring form). On phones it all folds into the menu button
- **Home**: hero ("Find Your Next Opportunity. Build Your Future.") with search by job title or skill, location and category, animated stats, **job categories** with live job counts, featured jobs (real openings first), an **employer section** (Post a Job, Find Candidates, Bulk Hiring, Permanent Recruitment, Contract Staffing), industry tabs, 6-stage process, testimonials, FAQ, blog preview and CTAs. Sample testimonials show until you add real ones (or hide the section) under Admin → Settings → Homepage testimonials, and the dashboard reminds you until then. Every page has a skip-to-content link for keyboard and screen-reader users
- **Job Categories** (`/categories`): 12 groups (Office jobs, Skilled & field jobs, IT & Engineering, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Logistics & Delivery, Construction, Retail, Hospitality, Sales, Finance & Banking, Fresher jobs) with open-job counts, each opening Find Jobs filtered to that group (`/jobs?group=…`), then every category listed by kind of work
- **Sample jobs**: the starter jobs that come with the site are examples, not real vacancies. They show a **Sample** label, can't be applied to, send no job alerts, and stay out of Google for Jobs and the sitemap. The dashboard reminds you to replace them; editing one in Admin → Jobs makes it a real job
- **Job search** (`/jobs` and the JSON API `GET /api/jobs`): every word of the keyword must appear in the title, skills, company, category or description, with title matches ranked first. Filter by city, **state** or remote, **several categories** at once, **experience level** (fresher, entry 0–2, mid 3–7, senior 8+ years, from each job's experience range), **minimum pay** (a month; daily and yearly pay are compared at 26 days and 12 months), job type and work arrangement, and sort by relevance, newest or highest pay. Results are in a stable order (date, then id), so pages never repeat or skip a job. When nothing matches, each active filter is shown with a link that removes it. `GET /api/jobs` takes the same filters (also as `keyword`, `city`, `state`, `employmentType=full_time…`, `workArrangement=remote…`), `page` and `limit` (20 by default, 50 at most), refuses bad values with a 400, returns only open, published jobs within their application period (no sample listings), and is limited to 120 requests a minute per IP; `GET /api/jobs/:id` returns one job
- **Resume safety**: every uploaded resume must really be a PDF, DOC or DOCX (its first bytes are checked, not just the name) and under 5 MB. Set `CLAMAV_HOST` (and `CLAMAV_PORT`, default 3310) to a ClamAV `clamd` and every resume is scanned before it is saved; infected files are refused and deleted, and if the scanner can't be reached uploads are refused rather than let through. Resume downloads (team, candidate and client) need the sign-in **and** a signed link from the page that expires after 15 minutes, so a copied or forwarded link stops working
- **One application per person per job**: besides the check when applying, the database itself refuses a second live application for the same person (email, or mobile number when there's no email) and job; withdrawn applications don't count. Duplicates made before this rule were marked once (the one furthest along is kept; the others show a **Duplicate** pill)
- **Job addresses**: jobs live at `/jobs/<title>-<city>-<id>` (e.g. `/jobs/full-stack-developer-mern-bengaluru-hr-101`), used in links, the sitemap, Google for Jobs data and share buttons. The id finds the job, so `/jobs/hr-101` still works (pointing search engines to the full address) and an address with an old title moves to the current one
- **Pay as numbers**: each job's pay is a minimum and/or maximum in rupees per month, year, day or hour, plus an optional note ("+ PF & ESI"), or left blank when not disclosed. The minimum can't exceed the maximum. Yearly pay of ₹1 lakh or more shows as LPA. Existing jobs' salary text was read into numbers once on upgrade
- **Job details**: workplace with its state, pay (or "Not disclosed"), experience, education, work schedule, qualifications, benefits, closing date, a **Verified employer** badge when the job is linked to an active client account, and **Report this job** (fake, asks for money, misleading, filled, discriminatory). Reports are emailed to the team and listed under Admin → Inbox → Reported jobs. A job past its closing date stops taking applications at once, even before the hourly check closes it
- **Find Jobs** (`/jobs`): office, skilled and field jobs side by side. Tabs for each **kind of work** (Office & professional, Skilled & technical, Industrial, field & service) with job counts, plus keyword, category (grouped by kind of work), location, work-mode (On-site, Hybrid, Remote, Field work, Client site) and job-type filters, job detail pages, and online applications with resume upload. **Quick apply** for skilled and field jobs: a name and mobile number are enough (email, resume and skills are optional extras), the recruiter calls back, and an **Apply on WhatsApp** button opens a chat with your team pre-filled with the job (shown once a real WhatsApp number is saved in Settings). Office jobs still ask for a resume and email, and the job form's **Resume** setting can switch any single job either way. People without an email are matched by mobile number (+91 98765 43210 and 09876543210 count as the same), so applying twice still updates one application, and the Talent pool and data retention treat each number as one person. Candidates without an email can't use My applications yet. If someone applies again for a job they already applied to, their existing application is updated with the new resume and details (stage, notes and history are kept) and the team is told, instead of a second candidate appearing. Someone who withdrew and re-applies gets a fresh application
- **Services** (`/services`): an overview with a "which one fits" guide, and a page for each of the six services (Permanent Staffing, Contract & Temp Staffing, Executive Search, Bulk & RPO Hiring, Background Verification, Corporate Training) covering what's included, who it suits, how it works, how billing works and FAQs. Each page has Google Service and FAQ structured data, and its "Share a requirement" button pre-selects the service on the hire form
- **Industries** (`/industries`): a page for each of the ten specialist desks with the roles hired, what recruiters screen for, related services and that desk's live open jobs. All service and industry wording lives in `catalog.js`, and the homepage, footer, hire form and sitemap follow it
- **Hire a Team** (`/hire`): employer requirement form, engagement models and the hiring process
- **Privacy Policy** (`/privacy`) and **Terms of Use** (`/terms`), written for India's DPDP Act 2023, linked from the footer and from every form that collects personal data. They pull your email, phone and address from Admin → Settings. Change `LEGAL_UPDATED` in `server.js` when you edit either page
- **About**, **Blog** (with articles) and **Contact** pages
- Newsletter signup, a floating WhatsApp button, and a mobile-responsive layout
- **Job alerts** (`/jobs`): candidates pick a category and/or city, confirm by email (double opt-in), then get an email the first time each matching job goes live. Remote jobs match every city. Every alert has a one-click unsubscribe, and subscribers are listed under Admin → Job alerts
- **Saved jobs** (`/saved`): a bookmark button on every job card and job page, with a count in the header. Saved jobs are stored in the visitor's browser, so no account is needed. Closed jobs stay listed and marked Closed, and deleted jobs drop off automatically
- **Spam protection**: every public form has a hidden bot trap and a per-IP limit (5–6 submissions per 10 minutes), and the admin locks out an IP for 15 minutes after 10 wrong passwords. Behind a reverse proxy, set `TRUST_PROXY=1` so limits apply per visitor
- **Share a job**: WhatsApp, LinkedIn, copy-link and the phone's share sheet on every open job, using the public `SITE_URL` address. Shared links show a branded **preview image**: each job gets its own (title, company, city, work mode, experience and salary), and every other page shows the site image with your tagline. Images are drawn on the server (`og.js`, fonts in `assets/fonts`) and change automatically when a job is edited
- **Job-seeker accounts** (`/account/register`, linked as **Register** in the header): name, email and mobile number (city, experience, notice period, skills, LinkedIn and a resume are optional), plus agreement to the terms. The welcome email has a link that opens the account (valid 24 hours), so only the owner of the address gets in; registering again with the same email updates the profile and gives the same reply, so the form can't reveal who has an account. A registration is stored as a general application with the source "Candidate account", so the team is emailed and sees new sign-ups in Applications and the Talent pool like any CV. Signed in, candidates get **one-click apply** on every job page using their saved profile (each application gets its own copy of the resume; office jobs need a resume on file first), **Jobs for you** (open jobs matching their skills, then their city or remote), **job alerts** they can add and remove (on at once, as the email is already confirmed), resume upload and replacement, and name and phone in the profile. Signed in, saved jobs are also kept in the account (`/api/candidate/saved-jobs`), merged with the ones on the device, so they follow the candidate to other devices; the header shows **My account** instead of Register
- **My account** (`/account`): candidates sign in with a one-time emailed link (no password) using the email they registered or applied with. They see each application's progress in plain language, upcoming interviews with join link and calendar file, update their profile (skills, experience, city, notice, LinkedIn) across all applications, withdraw an application (interviews are cancelled and the team is emailed), re-download the resume they sent with each application, **download a copy of their data** (a JSON file with their applications, details, progress, interviews, emails sent and job alerts, without recruiter notes), and delete all their data (applications, resumes, job alerts, newsletter)
- **Client portal** (`/employer`): clients log in to see their roles with applicant counts by stage, their placements with guarantee end dates and invoices (view, print or save as PDF), send new hiring requirements (they arrive in Admin → Hire requests and by email) and follow their status. Passwords are hashed with scrypt, invite/reset links are single-use and expire, and logins are rate-limited
- **SEO**: Google for Jobs structured data (JobPosting) on every open job, Organization/WebSite/BlogPosting data, `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt`, canonical and Open Graph tags. Closed jobs are removed from the sitemap and marked `noindex`
- **Admin panel** (`/admin`):
  - **Team logins**: everyone signs in at `/admin/login` with their own email and password; sessions last 12 hours and there's a Sign out link. Admins invite people from the **Team** tab (they get a one-time link to choose a password, valid for 3 days), change roles, resend links and deactivate accounts (which signs them out at once). **Admin** can do everything. **Recruiter** works jobs, candidates, interviews, shortlists and clients and records placements, but can't see or touch invoices, payments, billing totals, settings, the team, the activity log or backups, and can't delete records. The team always keeps at least one Admin, and 10 wrong passwords lock out an IP for 15 minutes
  - **Two-step sign-in**: anyone on the team can turn it on under **Sign-in security** (link at the top of the admin panel) by scanning a QR code with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy or any authenticator app. After their password they then enter the 6-digit code from the app. Each code works once, and turning it on signs out their other sessions. They get 10 single-use **recovery codes** (shown once, to print or save) for a lost phone, and can make new ones. Five wrong codes go back to the password step, and wrong codes count towards the IP lock-out. On the Team tab, Admins see who uses it, can **Reset two-step** for someone who lost their phone (which signs them out and emails them), and can **require it for the whole team** once they use it themselves. Anyone without it is then sent to set it up before they can use the admin panel. Everyone is emailed when it's turned on, turned off, reset, or a recovery code is used. If the owner account loses both its phone and its recovery codes and no other Admin can reset it, start the server once with `RESET_OWNER_2FA=1`
  - **Ownership**: each job has a recruiter owner, and its applicants belong to that owner unless you assign one directly on the application. Filter the Jobs and Applications tabs by Mine, Unassigned or any team member. Recruiters get a "My candidates" number on the dashboard
  - **Activity log** (Admins): every change made in the admin panel, with who made it and when (sign-ins, jobs posted, stage changes, invoices, payments, team changes). Filter it by person or export it as CSV. The latest 2,000 entries are kept
  - **Stage history & timeline**: every pipeline move is recorded with when, from, to and who, whether from the stage dropdown, bulk actions, booking an interview or recording a placement. Each application has a **Timeline** showing when they applied (and from where), stage moves, interviews, emails, client sharing and feedback, and withdrawal. The Applications CSV includes the history in readable form. Reports use the history for exact conversion figures and show the median first response (applied to first stage move)
  - **Reports**: choose a period (last 30 or 90 days, this or last financial year, last 12 months, all time) and see applications, placements and median time to hire (application to offer accepted). Charts show placements per month and, for Admins, billed per month, each with a table view. Also shows pipeline conversion (how far applications got), **source of hire**, and results by recruiter, job category and (Admins) top clients, each with a CSV download. Recruiters see the same reports without money. Source of hire comes from utm_source links (the WhatsApp, LinkedIn and copy-link share buttons and job-alert emails add one automatically) or the referring site, and is recorded with each new application
  - **Dashboard** (opens first): for Admins, a **Needs your attention** list when backups fail or while the phone, WhatsApp, address, social links, invoice details or email sending are still placeholders; then key numbers, a 14-day applications chart, the candidate pipeline funnel, most-applied jobs and recent activity
  - **Jobs**: post, edit, close/reopen and delete openings. Job types are Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Temporary, Daily wage / Gig, Apprenticeship and Internship. There are 30 categories, 14 of them for skilled, industrial and service work (Skilled Trades, Technicians & Field Service, Healthcare Technicians & Nursing, Factory & Production, Warehouse & Packing, Delivery & Driving, Construction & Site Work, Security Services, Housekeeping & Facility Services, Hotel & Restaurant Staff, Store & Counter Staff, Domestic Help & Caregiving, Events & Promotions, Agriculture & Farm Work). Each category belongs to a kind of work, and **Kind of work** on the job form overrides it for one job (e.g. a site helper listed under Construction & Real Estate). Categories you add in Settings count as office work unless a job says otherwise. Sites that had already saved their own category list got the new ones added once (removing them again in Settings sticks). Write salaries as `₹14 – 20 LPA`, `₹18,000 – 24,000 per month`, `₹800 per day` or `₹150 per hour` so Google for Jobs shows the pay with the right period. Closed jobs drop out of listings, keep their page, and stop taking applications. Give a job an optional **closing date** (the last day to apply, India time) and it closes by itself the day after: it leaves the listings and the sitemap, and its Google for Jobs `validThrough` matches the date. Job cards say "Closes in 3 days" in the final week. Jobs open 60+ days with no closing date are flagged on the Jobs tab and dashboard so filled roles don't linger. Reopening an expired job clears its old date
  - **Bulk actions**: tick candidates on the Applications tab (or tick the header box to select every candidate on the page), then in one go move them to a stage (optionally emailing them the stage update), assign an owner, email them with a template, or delete them with their resumes (Admins only). You go back to the same filtered view afterwards
  - **Email templates** (Templates tab): reusable emails with placeholders ({first_name}, {name}, {job}, {company}, {location}, {recruiter}, {site_name}, {site_phone}). Four ready-made ones are included, and anyone on the team can add or edit them (only Admins can delete). Before sending you see a preview for a real candidate and can edit the text. Each candidate gets their own copy, replies go to the recruiter who sent it, withdrawn candidates are skipped, and the application shows when it was last emailed and by whom
  - **Add candidate** (Applications tab): record someone who applied by WhatsApp message, phone call, walk-in, referral or a job fair, with a name and mobile number, optional email, resume, skills and recruiter notes. Nobody is emailed. If they already applied for that job (same email, or same number when there's no email) their application is updated and the notes added. How they reached you shows in their timeline and in the Reports' source of hire. Every candidate's row has call and WhatsApp links, and says when there's no email
  - **Applications pipeline**: move candidates through New → Screening → Interview → Offered → Hired / Rejected, add recruiter notes, filter by stage and download resumes
  - **Interviews**: book a video, phone or in-person interview from any application (India time). The candidate gets an email with a calendar invite (Accept/Decline in Gmail and Outlook) and moves to the Interview stage. The Interviews tab and dashboard show what is coming up; cancelling emails the candidate and removes it from their calendar. Each interview also has a downloadable .ics file for the interviewer. **Reminders** go out automatically about a day before and about an hour before, to the candidate (with the join link or address) and to the interviewer if their email was added. A reminder is skipped if the interview was booked too close to it, so it never arrives right after the booking email, and each reminder shows in the application's timeline
  - **Placements & invoicing**: when a candidate accepts an offer, click **Record placement** on their application to log the client, joining date, annual CTC and fee (a % of CTC or a fixed amount), and the replacement-guarantee period. The application moves to Hired. Track each placement as Awaiting joining → Joined (or Did not join / Left in guarantee). You can see who is still in their guarantee period, then issue a numbered GST invoice (`INV/2026-27/0001`, restarting each April) with CGST + SGST or IGST, the SAC code and the amount in words. Print it or save it as a PDF, and email it to a portal client. On each invoice's **Payments** page, record every payment with the amount received, the TDS the client deducted (pre-filled at your usual rate), the mode and the UTR/cheque reference. Invoices move from Unpaid to Part paid to Paid automatically, and overdue ones are flagged. Issue a numbered GST **credit note** (`CN/2026-27/0001`) when a candidate doesn't join, leaves in the guarantee period or the fee changes. It reverses GST at the invoice's rate, reduces the balance, and shows a refund due if the client has already paid. Issued invoices are never changed or deleted. An invoice with no payments can be cancelled and reissued (the old number stays on record). Once money or a credit note is recorded, corrections go through credit notes. The Placements tab shows the amount received and the TDS to claim for the financial year, and the CSV export adds received, TDS, credited, balance and refund columns. When you send an overpayment back, record it as a **refund** on the Payments page. Send a **payment reminder** email to portal clients from the Payments page or the placements list, or turn on automatic reminders in Settings (once a week, at most 4 per overdue invoice). When a candidate does not join or leaves in the guarantee period, **Record replacement** lets you pick the replacement hire (applicants for the same job are listed first). The client, billing details and guarantee carry over, the replacement is free by default, and both placements link to each other. **Contract staffing** placements can use **Monthly billing**: set the monthly bill rate and an optional contract end date. Then **Bill a month** issues one invoice per month (SAC 998513) with the deployed dates. Partial months are pro-rated by days (half days allowed), and you can add or deduct an adjustment such as overtime or unpaid leave. Each monthly invoice has its own payments, TDS, credit notes, reminders and cancel option. The Placements tab lists months still to bill, the Contracts filter shows active contracts, and the dashboard flags any finished month that has not been billed. **Contractor timesheets**: contractors on monthly-billed contracts sign in to My applications and log each week (full day, half day or off per date). Days outside the contract or in the future are blocked. The client approves the week, or sends it back with a note, in the client portal, and the contractor is emailed either way. For clients not on the portal the team is emailed and an Admin approves on the Bill a month page. Approved weeks are locked. Bill a month shows approved and pending days, pre-fills billable days from approved timesheets, and can bill by working days (rate × days ÷ Mon–Fri days in the month) or calendar days. The **Clients** tab shows each client's placements, amount billed, amount settled and amount outstanding. Your GSTIN, PAN, bank details, default fee %, guarantee days, GST rate and payment terms go under **Settings → Billing & invoices**. The dashboard shows billed this financial year, outstanding amounts and a follow-up list (joinings this week, guarantees ending, invoices to raise, overdue payments). Placements are kept even if a candidate deletes their data, because invoices are tax records
  - **Interview feedback**: a scorecard for each interview with an overall rating (1–5), a recommendation (Strong hire, Hire, No hire, Strong no hire), strengths and concerns. Anyone on the team can add one (one per person, editable). Add the interviewer's email when booking and they get the details plus a private no-login feedback link. It shows the candidate's name and role only (no contact details), works until two weeks after the interview, and stops working if the interview is cancelled. The team is emailed when they submit. The Interviews tab lists **Feedback due** and each interview's average score and majority recommendation (ties go to the latest scorecard). Applications show the latest verdict, feedback appears in the timeline, and the dashboard flags feedback due
  - **Clients**: add a client company and contact; they get an invite link to set a password for the **client portal** (`/employer/login`). Link jobs to a client from the job form. Resend invites or reset links, and deactivate a client to sign them out everywhere
  - **Shortlists**: share any candidate with a client (with a recruiter note). The client sees the profile, skills, match score and resume, but never the candidate's email or phone, and answers Interested / Maybe / Not a fit with a comment, which shows on the application and is emailed to the team
  - **Resume reading**: on the apply form the resume field comes first. When a candidate picks a PDF or Word (.docx) resume, the site reads it and fills in any empty fields (name, email, phone, LinkedIn, experience, key skills), never overwriting what they typed. Nothing is stored until they apply. Each application keeps the resume text, so the **Talent pool** search also looks inside resumes, and skills found in the resume count towards the skill match (shown as "Also in resume"). Older resumes can be read with one click on the Talent tab (Admins). Old .doc files and scanned (image-only) PDFs can't be read and are simply skipped
  - **Skill match**: candidates list their key skills when applying; each application shows a % match against the job's required skills (hover for matched/missing) and can be sorted by best match
  - **Talent pool**: one searchable record per candidate across all their applications. Search by name, skill, city, job or notes, filter by experience, notice period and stage, and rank the whole pool against any job to fill new roles from past applicants
  - **Blog**: write, edit and delete articles (title, link, category, date, summary, paragraphs) and keep drafts hidden until they are ready
  - **Backups** (Settings, Admins): the database is backed up automatically every day (and on start-up if the last copy is a day old). Each copy is integrity-checked, the newest 14 are kept, and any of them can be downloaded; **Back up now** takes one on demand. If a backup fails or none has run for 2 days, the dashboard says so. Copies go to a `backups` folder next to the database; set `BACKUP_DIR` to another disk or a synced folder (e.g. Google Drive or OneDrive) so one failed disk can't take both. Resumes live in the uploads folder, so include that in your server backups
  - **Data retention** (Settings, Admins, off by default): delete candidates automatically after 12, 24 or 36 months without activity (applying, stage moves, interviews, emails, client sharing), with their resumes and sign-in links, plus contact messages and hiring requests older than the same period and job alerts never confirmed within 30 days. Each option shows how many candidates it would delete before you save. Candidates with an active placement or an upcoming interview are kept, and placements, invoices, confirmed job alerts and newsletter subscribers are never touched. It runs daily, every clean-up is in the activity log, and the Privacy Policy states the period automatically
  - **Settings**: edit phone, WhatsApp, email, address, hours, tagline, social links, the homepage stats and up to 6 homepage testimonials (quote, name or role, company, star rating). Changes go live instantly
  - Hiring requests, contact messages and newsletter signups, with delete on every entry
  - **Pagination**: long admin tables (applications, talent pool, jobs, placements, activity log, requests, messages, subscribers) show 50 rows per page, and Find Work shows 20 jobs per page. Filters, sorting and owner views are kept when you change page, ticking the header box in Applications selects that page, and CSV exports always include every row
  - **Export CSV** on every tab (Excel-ready)
- **Email notifications**: your team is alerted on every application, hiring request and contact message (Reply-To is the sender), candidates get an "application received" email, and recruiters can email a candidate when they move to Interview, Offered, Hired or Rejected. Every email is sent with a plain-text version alongside the HTML, for text-only mail apps and better spam scores

## Run it
```bash
npm install
npm start            # http://localhost:3004
```
To enable the admin panel, set a password first:
```powershell
$env:ADMIN_PASS="choose-a-strong-password"; npm start   # PowerShell
```
Then open http://localhost:3004/admin/login and sign in as `admin` with that password. This is the built-in **owner** account (an Admin), and you can change the username with `ADMIN_USER`. From the **Team** tab, invite your recruiters so each person has their own login.

## Tests
```bash
npm test
```
Unit tests cover the billing maths (GST split, amount in words, financial years, payments, TDS, credit notes and refunds), settings and blog validation, skill matching, resume reading, stage history, timesheets and SEO data. End-to-end tests start the real server with a throwaway database, email outbox and uploads folder (your `data/` is never touched) and walk through applying, spam protection, admin sign-in and cross-site protection, closing a job, candidate sign-in links, and recording a placement through to a paid invoice. Run them before every deploy.

**Access control:** [`docs/ACCESS.md`](docs/ACCESS.md) lists every route and who may use it (public, one-time link, candidate, client, team, Admin). `test/access.test.js` reads the routes from the code and fails if that file is out of date, and checks that signed-out visitors and Recruiters are refused wherever they should be. When you add a route, add it to the table. [`docs/AUDIT.md`](docs/AUDIT.md) is the platform audit and backlog for the all-industry plan (office, skilled and field jobs).

## Email setup
Without SMTP settings, every email is saved to `data/outbox/*.eml` (open these in Outlook or any mail app to preview them). To send real email, set these before `npm start`:

| Variable | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `SMTP_HOST` | `smtp.gmail.com` | Turns on real sending |
| `SMTP_PORT` | `587` | `465` uses SSL |
| `SMTP_USER` / `SMTP_PASS` | `you@gmail.com` / app password | Gmail needs an App Password |
| `MAIL_FROM` | `"Hireriv" <jobs@hireriv.com>` | Defaults to the site email in Admin → Settings |
| `NOTIFY_EMAIL` | `recruiters@hireriv.com` | Where team alerts go; defaults to the site email |
| `SITE_URL` | `https://hireriv.com` | Your live domain: used in emails, canonical URLs, the sitemap and job structured data. **Set it in production.** |

```powershell
$env:SMTP_HOST="smtp.gmail.com"; $env:SMTP_USER="you@gmail.com"; $env:SMTP_PASS="app-password"; $env:ADMIN_PASS="secret"; npm start
```

## Data & backups
Everything the site stores (jobs, blog posts, settings, applications, requests, messages, subscribers, client accounts and sign-in sessions) lives in one SQLite database, `data/hireriv.db`, using the SQLite built into Node.js (22.13 or newer), so there is nothing extra to install. Resumes are saved in `uploads/`.

- **First start:** the database is created and filled from `data/jobs.json`, `data/posts.json` and `data/site.json`. Any older `data/submissions/*.json` files are imported once, and the originals are left untouched.
- **Backups:** the database is backed up automatically every day to a `backups` folder next to it (or `BACKUP_DIR`), with the newest 14 copies kept and listed under Admin → Settings → Backups. **Download database backup** there gives a copy on demand. Resumes in `uploads/` are not in the database, so back that folder up too.
- **Location:** set `DB_FILE` to keep the database somewhere else (e.g. a mounted disk).
- Client and candidate sign-ins are stored in the database, so restarting the server keeps everyone signed in.

## Go-live checklist
1. **Server settings** (`.env` or your host's dashboard): `SITE_URL` (https), `ADMIN_PASS`, `SMTP_HOST` / `SMTP_USER` / `SMTP_PASS`, `MAIL_FROM`, `NOTIFY_EMAIL`, `TRUST_PROXY=1` behind a proxy, and `BACKUP_DIR` on a second disk or synced folder.
2. **Persistent storage** for the database, `uploads/` and backups (see Deploy).
3. **Admin → Settings:** real phone, WhatsApp, address and social links; legal name, GSTIN, PAN and bank details; your fee, guarantee, GST and TDS defaults; and decide on **Data retention**. The dashboard's **Needs your attention** list shows what is still missing.
4. **Content:** replace the sample jobs, the homepage stats and the testimonials with real ones, and review the three starter blog articles.
5. **Legal:** have a lawyer review `/privacy` and `/terms` (the text is in `views/privacy.ejs` and `views/terms.ejs`).
6. **Team:** invite your recruiters from the Team tab so nobody shares the owner login. Turn on two-step sign-in for yourself under Sign-in security, then require it for the team on the Team tab.
7. **Check:** run `npm test`, apply for a job with your own email, confirm the emails arrive, then share a job link on WhatsApp to see the preview image.

## Deploy
Hireriv is one Node.js process with a SQLite file, so any host that runs **Node 22.13+** with a **persistent disk** works. Keep the database and `uploads/` on that disk, and put the site behind HTTPS.

**Settings** – copy `.env.example` to `.env` and fill it in (`SITE_URL` with https, `ADMIN_PASS`, the `SMTP_*` values, `TRUST_PROXY=1` behind a proxy). Run with `npm run start:env`, or set the same variables in your host's dashboard. With `NODE_ENV=production` the server prints a warning at startup for anything important that is missing.

**Docker** (on a VPS):
```bash
docker build -t hireriv .
docker run -d --name hireriv -p 3004:3004 --env-file .env -v hireriv-data:/var/lib/hireriv --restart unless-stopped hireriv
```
The image keeps the database and resumes in the `hireriv-data` volume and checks `/healthz` every 30 seconds. Put Nginx or Caddy in front for HTTPS.

**Render / Railway** – create a Node web service from this repo (build: `npm ci`, start: `node server.js`), attach a persistent disk mounted at e.g. `/var/data`, and set `DB_FILE=/var/data/hireriv.db`, `UPLOADS_DIR=/var/data/uploads`, `TRUST_PROXY=1` plus the settings above. Use `/healthz` as the health check path.

**Built in:** security headers (Content-Security-Policy with no inline scripts, anti-framing, nosniff, referrer and permissions policies, HSTS on https), `/healthz` (checks the database), private areas hidden from search engines, and cache-busted CSS/JS.
